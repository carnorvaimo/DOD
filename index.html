How I see the opportunity

Use the consolidation to make us better at delivery, not just different at delivery.

We are too small to operate as three separate organisations indefinitely. The consolidation gives us an opportunity to bring expertise together, get closer to our clients and operate more deliberately as one Delivery organisation.

What I see as the opportunity

Pool expertise

Bring together market-specific initiatives, align remaining variations and build stronger shared capabilities.

Create clarity

Make it easier to understand what good delivery looks like and how we work.

Reduce unnecessary variation

Where market-specific versions don't add value, build one good standard.

Build capability

Use the scale of one organisation to support stronger Delivery Leaders and better capacity decisions.

But not one-size-fits-all

Different verticals, clients and project types will have legitimate differences.

Standardise what creates value. Adapt where context matters.

My ambition

People shouldn't have to guess what good delivery looks like, and good delivery shouldn't require unnecessary process.

Slide 04 / 06

1. Make good delivery easier

PMs should have:

  • • Clear expectations
  • • Simple processes
  • • Useful tools
  • • A clear AI strategy to remove low-value work
  • • Strong peer support
  • • Less administrative work that doesn't improve delivery

Where we have three ways of doing something, I'd ask:

"Do we actually need three?"

2. Make delivery more predictable

I want us to become better at answering:

"What can we realistically commit to, with what capability, and where are the risks?"

Not just reporting capacity, using it to make better decisions.

3. Make improvement actually improve things

Continue the work we've started with Flow, project health and value-based delivery.

But keep asking: Did this improve delivery or value?

Not simply: Did everyone adopt the process?

Build in a regular check with the CEDs, Capacity planning manager and delivery people manager: what's shown up since the new structure launched, what's dropped. This should be regular and frequent in the beginning to be able to happen less frequent as the new org has settled.

Use AI where it removes low-value work: “You shouldn't be doing X manually anymore.”

Including my own. If I'm asking teams to work differently, I should hold myself to the same standard, not just direct it at others.

4. Build stronger Delivery Leaders

The goal isn't simply that people have a PM / Programme Manager / Delivery Manager title.

They should know:

  • • What good looks like
  • • What they need to improve
  • • Where to get support
  • • How to learn from each other
