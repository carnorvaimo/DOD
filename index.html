If I started tomorrow

First focus: understand, connect, stabilise

Slide 02 / 05
01

Understand & align

  • • Align with the three vertical leaders on their vision, priorities and expectations for Delivery.
  • • Establish an effective working rhythm with L and A early.
  • • Talk to PMs and Delivery Managers to understand ground reality: gaps, challenges, risks and emerging drift.
  • • Build a view of what is genuinely different between verticals — and what doesn't need to be.
02

Stabilise & protect

  • • Give additional attention to teams experiencing the most change, while allowing others time to settle.
  • • Reinforce critical routines already underway, rather than introducing unnecessary new ones.
  • • Audit ongoing initiatives: Map ownership and progress, push to finish where there is clear value, and sunset where there isn't.
  • • Make sure valuable work doesn't become invisible or wasted.
03

Create clarity

  • • Start identifying where we need common standards and expectations across Delivery.
  • • Reduce unnecessary variations and duplicated ways of working.
  • • Keep processes simple and useful — particularly for PMs and Delivery Managers.

Principle

"Don't change everything because the organisation changed. First understand what needs to change, what needs to continue, and what should stop."

One Delivery organisation

  • • Shared expertise rather than duplicated capability
  • • Consistent principles and expectations
  • • Flexibility where vertical or client context genuinely requires it

PMs enabled to do great work

  • • PMs don't have to guess how to do their job well
  • • Simple processes and useful tools
  • • Strong peer support and access to expertise
  • • Less administrative overhead

Continuous improvement

  • • Continue the execution of Flow
  • • Shift focus from process adoption toward value delivery
  • • Continue developing project health and delivery visibility
  • • Build AI into everyday practice where it removes manual work
  • • Improve cycle time and mature value-based delivery

Stronger Delivery × Engineering partnership

  • • Work more closely with Engineering Director and Managers
  • • Solve systemic delivery issues together rather than optimising functions separately
Over time, I want to see this structural evolution across all teams.
