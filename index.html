Delivery Operations Director

How I see the opportunity

The Opportunity

We are moving from three market-based organisations toward a more focused business, closer to our clients.

For me, the opportunity is not simply to consolidate Operations. It is to build on what we have and become better as one Delivery organisation.

We Can

We are small enough that we shouldn't need three versions of everything. We can:

  • • Pool expertise and build stronger shared capabilities
  • • Create more clarity and consistency
  • • Reduce unnecessary variations and duplicated effort
  • • Learn from each other across verticals
  • • Keep the flexibility needed for different markets, clients and project types

My Ambition

PMs shouldn't have to guess how to do their job well.

The job shouldn't necessarily be easy — but people should know what good looks like, have the tools and support to achieve it, and be able to learn from the people around them.

And our processes should help people deliver, not create work about the work.

If I got the job tomorrow

Listen. Connect. Stabilise. Create clarity.

I wouldn't start by introducing a new operating model. I'd start by understanding what the new organisation needs.

01

Understand the direction

  • • Align with the three vertical leaders on their vision, priorities and expectations from Delivery
  • • Establish the working rhythm with L and A
  • • Understand where they see opportunities, risks and gaps
02

Understand the reality

  • • Talk to PMs and Delivery Managers
  • • Understand where delivery is getting stuck, where there is drift and where change has created uncertainty
  • • Spend more attention on teams experiencing the most change
  • • Let areas that are working settle rather than changing things for the sake of change
03

Protect & simplify

  • • Audit ongoing delivery initiatives from the previous markets
  • • Map ownership, progress and remaining value
  • • Make deliberate push-to-finish vs. sunset decisions
  • • Make sure good work doesn't become invisible or wasted just because the org chart changed around it
  • • Reinforce important initiatives already underway rather than creating another wave of activity

My Principle

"Don't change everything because the organisation changed. Understand what needs to change, what needs to continue — and what should stop."

What I bring

Clarity: This is probably my strongest contribution. I enjoy taking complexity or ambiguity and making it clearer and easier to act on.
Organisational knowledge: I've moved through different roles and have a good understanding of our processes, tools, people and challenges.
Delivery experience: I understand both how things are supposed to work and where reality doesn't always match the process.
Relationships & trust: I have an existing network across the organisation that gives me a strong starting point for change.
Pragmatism: I care about consistency and quality, but I'm wary of creating administration for its own sake.
Curiosity: At the core, I'm excited about learning new things and getting better. This role would be a chance to broaden that further.
