Delivery operations director Slide 01 / 06

My view of the role

The organisational change is ultimately about getting closer to our clients. Delivery Operations should make that easier, not become another layer between the business and the client

The opportunity

We are moving from three market-based organisations toward one more focused business, closer to our clients.

For me, the opportunity is not simply to consolidate Operations. It is to build on what we have and become better as one Delivery organisation.

Why now & what we can do

We are small enough that we shouldn't need three versions of everything. We can use this opportunity to:

  • • Pool expertise instead of solving the same problems three times
  • • Create clarity around how we deliver and build consistency where it improves quality and efficiency
  • • Reduce unnecessary variations and duplicated effort
  • • Learn from each other across verticals and develop stronger capabilities
  • • Keep the flexibility needed for different markets, clients and project types

My ambition

PMs shouldn't have to guess how to do their job well.

The job shouldn't necessarily be easy, but people should have the clarity, tools and support to achieve it, and be able to learn from the people around them.

And our processes should help people deliver, not create work about the work.

Listen first. Then act.

Slide 04 / 06

Stay close to the ground

The CEDs and Delivery Leaders see every day what is actually working on live accounts. I want a genuine two-way feedback loop:

Reality What is happening?
Signal Data + teams
Action What changes?
Improve Did it work?

CEDs provide the signal from the ground. My role is to turn that signal into improvements and close the loop.

Hold the bar

When something isn't working, I want to understand the actual cause, not just the symptom.

  • • Process isn't followed: Is the process unclear or broken?
  • • Project health is poor: Is the problem capability, ownership, capacity or execution?
  • • Compliance slips: Is the expectation clear and practical?
  • • Change hasn't landed: Did we communicate, train and support it properly?

Setting a standard only matters if someone makes sure it is met.
